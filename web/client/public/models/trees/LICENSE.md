# Tree models — license & attribution

`NormalTree_1.glb`, `NormalTree_4.glb`, `BirchTree_1.glb`, `PineTree_1.glb`,
`PineTree_3.glb` are from **Quaternius — Ultimate Stylized Nature Pack**
(https://quaternius.com), released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)**:
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The FBX sources + textures were obtained from the mirror
github.com/walterpalladino/godot-quaternius-ultimate-stylized-nature (repo
scaffolding MIT © Walter H. Palladino; the models/textures themselves are
Quaternius CC0), then converted to GLB in Blender with the leaf/bark textures
downscaled to 512px and the meshes decimated to ~1.2k triangles per tree for
instanced rendering. Committed verbatim into the repo (no CDN hotlinking).

Credit: Quaternius (quaternius.com). Foliage tinting and canopy shading are
applied at render time in `lib/scene3d.ts`.
